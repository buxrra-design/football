Daily session summary

One block per played date; tap "Session summary" to collapse or expand it (the newest is open). Teams are ranked by points (win 3, draw 1, loss 0), then goal difference, then goals scored. Win % = wins ÷ matches that team played that day. Main team = the team a player played most matches for that day. Each team is shown as a squad (main players, with chemistry lines from all-time results). A team with fewer main players than the biggest team borrows its top outside contributor as a shaded Guest GK card in goal (caption: his matches for the team out of the team's matches); other players who played only a few matches for the team are listed under it as "Also played", (x/y) = matches for this team / total matches that day. Top duo/trio = for each team colour, the 2 / 3 players with the most goals + assists scored while playing together on that team. "Player stats this session" lists games, wins, goals, assists, clean sheets (🧤 = goalkeeper's team conceded 0), man of the match and win % per player; click a column header to sort. The stats table and the "Session summary" label are not included in screenshots.

Player ranking

How the rating works (40–99, like FUT). Every match counts with a recency weight: a match from the latest session counts fully, and the weight halves every 8 sessions back (no hard cut-off, so form counts more but nothing jumps when a session drops out).
Overall = 55% attack + 22% impact + 13% result + 10% defence. With man-of-the-match votes recorded: 50% attack + 20% impact + 12% result + 10% defence + 8% man of the match. The total is slightly reduced for players with fewer than 5 games (⏳ provisional). A player who played in goal gets up to 40% of the rating from goals conceded while in goal.
• Attack: goals + 0.7 × assists per match, scaled to the best player. Goals in close matches (margin ≤ 1) count a bit more than in blowouts (margin ≥ 4). Players with few matches are pulled towards the group average.
• Impact: ridge regression over every team-match. It estimates how many more goals a team scores, and how many fewer it concedes, when this player is on the pitch, after removing his teammates' share. Because teams are mixed every session, players can be told apart; the estimate is pulled towards neutral for few matches (±0.6 goals per match = full scale).
• Result: 60% result (win 1, draw ½) + 40% goal difference per match (capped at ±3) of the player's team, adjusted per match for how strong the opponents were compared with his teammates, and pulled towards the middle for small samples.
• Defence (DEF): 50% defensive impact (the ridge estimate of goals conceded with him on the pitch), 20% clean-sheet rate, 15% goals conceded per match against the group average, 15% goalkeeper record (only for players who played in goal; otherwise that share goes to the other three). Every part is recency-weighted and pulled towards the group average for few matches.
• Card stats: SHO = goals per match, PAS = assists per match (both recency-weighted, scaled to the best player), DEF = the defence rating above. Team generation, the equality panel and swap suggestions balance Overall (40%), SHO, PAS, DEF (20%) and Impact (10%).
• 🛡 Likely defender (automatic, shown in the breakdown): at least 6 games, a low attack share and a better-than-average defensive impact. Likely defenders are preferred in the back row of squads. Tap a card to see the full breakdown and the change since the last session.
Colors: Bronze ≤64, Silver 65–74, Gold ≥75. Rare (shiny card): played at least 50% of the last 5 match days (needs 2+ match days). 📋 TOTW (black): the 5 best-rated players of the last session (most recent match day). 🏆 TOTY (dark blue): after 15 January, the 5 best-rated players of the previous year. TOTW, TOTY and the awards use the plain average of the matches in their period, without the recency weights. Priority: TOTY › TOTW › base.
👑 Ballon d'Or (glowing gold shield-shaped card with a shine; 👑 + 👟 together give a shield card with a gold-to-fire foil and rotating light): only one player. Awarded on 15 October to the best-rated player of that year's games, shown on the card for a year until the next 15 October. 👟 Golden Boot (orange-red foil card with slanted cut corners and a shine): same schedule, the player with the most goals that year.
Table columns: Games = all / last 5 match days, Goals and Assists = all / last 5 match days, Δ = rating change since the previous session.
