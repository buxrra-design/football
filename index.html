Daily session summary

One block per played date. Win % = wins ÷ matches that team played that day. Best duo/trio = players who were on the same team, ranked by points per match (win 3, draw 1), then matches together, then combined goals + assists.

Player ranking

Rating 40–99 like FUT: goals (SHO), assists (PAS), clean sheets (DEF), per match, 40% all time + 60% last 5 match days.
Colors: Bronze ≤64, Silver 65–74, Gold ≥75. ✨ Rare (shiny): not played in the last 4 match days. 📋 TOTW (black): top 3 form over last 5 days. ⭐ Future Star (purple/pink): selected in Players tab. 🏆 TOTY (dark blue): after 15 January, top 5 rated + best goalkeeper (the player outside the top 5 with the most clean sheets, then highest rating) of the previous year. Priority: TOTY › Future Star › TOTW › base.
