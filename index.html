Game history

Daily session summary

One block per played date. Win % = wins ÷ matches that team played that day. Best duo/trio = players who were on the same team, ranked by points per match (win 3, draw 1), then matches together, then combined goals + assists.

Player ranking

Rating 40–99 like FUT: goals (SHO), assists (PAS), tackles (DEF), clean sheets (GK), per match, 60% all time + 40% last 5 match days. Overall = 30% SHO, 30% PAS, 20% DEF, 20% GK.
Colors: Bronze ≤64, Silver 65–74, Gold ≥75. ✨ Rare (shiny): not played in the last 4 match days. 📋 TOTW (black): same selection as TOTY (2 best scorers, best assister, 2 best tacklers, best clean sheets GK) but from the last session (most recent match day) only. ⭐ Future Star (purple/pink): selected in Players tab. 🏆 TOTY (dark blue): after 15 January, previous year's 2 best scorers, best assister, 2 best tacklers and best clean sheets GK (a player already picked is skipped, the next best takes the slot). Priority: TOTY › Future Star › TOTW › base.
👑 Ballon d'Or: only one player. Awarded on 15 October to the best-rated player of that year's games, shown on the card for a year until the next 15 October. 👟 Golden Boot: same schedule, the player with the most goals that year.
