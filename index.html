Game history

Daily session summary

One block per played date. Teams are ranked by points (win 3, draw 1, loss 0), then goal difference, then goals scored. Win % = wins ÷ matches that team played that day. Main team = the team a player played most matches for that day. Each team is shown as a squad (main players, with chemistry lines from all-time results); players who played only a few matches for the team are listed under it as "Also played", (x/y) = matches for this team / total matches that day. Top duo/trio = for each team colour, the 2 / 3 players with the most goals + assists scored while playing together on that team.

Player ranking

Rating 40–99 like FUT, from three stats: goals per match (SHO), assists per match (PAS) and team impact (WIN: 60% win rate with win = 1, draw = ½, plus 40% goal difference per match of the player's team). Each stat = 60% all time + 40% last 5 match days (a player with no games in the last 5 match days uses his all-time value for that 40%). SHO and PAS are scaled to the best player; WIN is the team impact, pulled slightly towards the middle for players with few games. Overall = 80% attack (goals + 0.7 × assists per match, so a goal counts a bit more than an assist) + 20% WIN, slightly reduced for players with fewer than 5 games.
Colors: Bronze ≤64, Silver 65–74, Gold ≥75. ✨ Rare (shiny): not played in the last 4 match days. 📋 TOTW (black): the 5 best-rated players of the last session (most recent match day). ⭐ Future Star (purple/pink): selected in Players tab, or a new player without games. 🏆 TOTY (dark blue): after 15 January, the 5 best-rated players of the previous year. Priority: TOTY › Future Star › TOTW › base.
👑 Ballon d'Or: only one player. Awarded on 15 October to the best-rated player of that year's games, shown on the card for a year until the next 15 October. 👟 Golden Boot: same schedule, the player with the most goals that year.
