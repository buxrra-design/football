Game history

Daily session summary

One block per played date. Teams are ranked by points (win 3, draw 1, loss 0), then goal difference, then goals scored. Win % = wins ÷ matches that team played that day. Main team = the team a player played most matches for that day. Each team is shown as a squad (main players, with chemistry lines from all-time results); players who played only a few matches for the team are listed under it as "Also played", (x/y) = matches for this team / total matches that day. Top duo/trio = for each team colour, the 2 / 3 players with the most goals + assists scored while playing together on that team.

Player ranking

Rating 40–99 like FUT, from three stats: goals per match (SHO), assists per match (PAS) and team impact (WIN: 60% win rate with win = 1, draw = ½, plus 40% goal difference per match of the player's team). Each stat = 60% all time + 40% last 5 match days (a player with no games in the last 5 match days uses his all-time value for that 40%). SHO and PAS are scaled to the best player; WIN is the team impact, pulled slightly towards the middle for players with few games and adjusted per match for how strong the opponents were compared with the player's teammates (beating a stronger team counts more). Overall = 80% attack (goals + 0.7 × assists per match, so a goal counts a bit more than an assist; goals in close matches count a little more than in blowouts; players with few matches are pulled towards the group average) + 20% WIN (with man-of-the-match votes recorded: 72% attack + 20% WIN + 8% man of the match; a player who played in goal gets up to 40% of the rating from goals conceded while in goal), slightly reduced for players with fewer than 5 games (shown as provisional). Tap a card to see the breakdown and the change since the last session.
Colors: Bronze ≤64, Silver 65–74, Gold ≥75. ✨ Rare (shiny): played at least 50% of the last 5 match days. 📋 TOTW (black): the 5 best-rated players of the last session (most recent match day). 🏆 TOTY (dark blue): after 15 January, the 5 best-rated players of the previous year. Priority: TOTY › TOTW › base.
👑 Ballon d'Or (glowing gold shield-shaped card with a shine; 👑 + 👟 together give a shield card with a gold-to-fire foil and rotating light): only one player. Awarded on 15 October to the best-rated player of that year's games, shown on the card for a year until the next 15 October. 👟 Golden Boot (orange-red foil card with slanted cut corners and a shine): same schedule, the player with the most goals that year.
