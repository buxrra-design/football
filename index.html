Rating and awards

Overall: 55% attack (goals + 0.7 × assists), 22% team impact, 13% result and 10% DEF. Result combines 60% win/draw and 40% normalised goal difference, adjusted for team strength. Goalkeeper games contribute a direct goals-conceded blend of up to 40%; goalkeeper record also affects DEF. DEF and impact are team-based estimates, not measured defensive actions. 🛡 is a defender guess.

Overall blends 60% all-time and 40% last five session days; without a recent appearance it uses all-time only. Both periods use recency weighting, halving every eight sessions. Sessions are calendar days, with partial attendance reducing influence. One long match is treated as a full session; clean-sheet rates remain imperfectly comparable. ⏳: −5 points for one session, −2 for two.

TOTW selects the highest-rated eligible players from each session. To qualify, a player must appear in more than half of their main team's matches; ties between main teams go to the most recently played for. Fewer than five may qualify. TOTY uses the previous year; Ballon d'Or and Golden Boot use games through 15 October. These period ratings weight sessions equally, without L5 blending. Yearly awards require attendance at 25% of sessions, rounded up.
